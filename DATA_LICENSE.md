Zenodo records (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23021720, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23005024)
are released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International license (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The source code is released separately under the MIT license (see LICENSE).

The data integrate records from third-party resources, including GPCRdb, ChEMBL,
PubChem, GTEx, the Human Protein Atlas, SIDER, OFFSIDES and openFDA, with
DrugBank identifiers and annotations. These original data remain subject to
the licenses and terms of their providers. Users should consult those terms
before reuse. The raw DrugBank dataset is not redistributed.
